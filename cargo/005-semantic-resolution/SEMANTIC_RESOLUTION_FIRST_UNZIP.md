# Cargo 005 — Semantic Resolution First Unzip

## Provenance

Source pressure:

`bonoj/Crucible/research/SEMANTIC_TINKERER_EXPEDITION.md`

Crucible remains authoritative for Semantic Tinkering, its candidate operations, multiscale fabrication, transition vocabulary, and the Foundry War load.

This cargo does **not** attempt to transfer that expedition wholesale. It reforms only the context-logistics pressure exposed by it.

## New pressure

Semantic Tinkering proposed that one entity may remain coherent across orders of magnitude while the amount and kind of executable reality changes with attention.

A drone may be a cube at one scale, a low-poly body at another, a richer articulated machine deeper in, and eventually expose an executable control system on its own surface.

The useful Unzip City analogy is not visual.

Working context may have the same property.

A receiving model may initially need only a coarse but truthful orientation surface. Pressure may then require descent into one semantic region: a mechanism, chronology tail, failure, source file, neighboring repository, or executable observation. After resolving that local need, the receiver may be able to return to a compact working representation that now carries the consequence of the descent without carrying every detail encountered there.

Candidate loop:

**coarse orientation → attention/need → semantic inpaint → source encounter → consequential update → semantic compaction**

This is adjacent to Semantic JIT but adds a useful emphasis: the successive representations may be understood as **different consequential resolutions of the same recoverable context**, not merely a summary followed by retrieval.

## Inpainting and outpainting as context operations

These are borrowed working handles, not a proposed API.

**Context inpainting** increases resolution inside an already identified semantic boundary.

Examples:

- a compact expedition result expands into the failure sequence that earned it;
- an entity-level semantic truth expands into the source systems that implement it;
- a current frontier expands into the chronology tail required to understand why it is unresolved;
- a repository-level capability expands into the neighboring repository whose authority it depends on.

**Context outpainting** extends beyond the current boundary when local material proves insufficient.

Examples:

- a Crucible mechanism exposes a dependency on FUUTP;
- a local expedition exposes relevant Clara continuity evidence;
- a project-level question expands into the repository graph needed to answer it.

Neither operation implies bulk ingestion. The pressure should determine how far resolution expands.

## Round-trip pressure

Cargo 002 established that a small orientation surface can cause selective deeper recovery.

The stronger round-trip question now visible is:

> **After descending into richer source material, can the receiver compact outward again while preserving the consequence that justified the descent?**

The result should not be a transcript of everything retrieved.

For example, a receiver may descend through a semantic surface into an implementation failure, discover that an apparent capability is only provisional, and return to its coarse working model carrying the corrected authority boundary. The detailed debugging chronology can remain source-local.

This resembles the Tinkerer invariant:

> **Preserve continuity of consequence. Make continuity of representation optional.**

For context logistics, a candidate translation is:

> **Preserve authority, provenance, and consequential revision. Allow working representation to change with attention.**

This is not yet earned as a universal rule.

## Different resolution axes

Context resolution need not be one-dimensional.

A working representation might independently require more resolution in:

- chronology;
- implementation;
- provenance;
- authority;
- failure evidence;
- repository topology;
- executable behavior;
- collaboration history.

Do not turn this list into a schema. It only demonstrates why “more context” is too crude a description.

## Relationship to prior cargo

Cargo 002 already demonstrated:

**small entry point → derived projection → destination authority → present semantic truth → bounded chronology tail → legitimate action boundary**

Cargo 005 does not claim a new mechanism. It changes how that successful crossing can be interpreted and what a future crossing could test.

Cargo 003 asked whether collaboration-pattern sufficiency is possible without chronology sufficiency.

Cargo 004 preserved cross-context residue while leaving physical machinery and unresolved engineering seams local.

Cargo 005 asks whether **semantic resolution itself can be malleable and round-trippable** while authority and provenance remain intact.

## What does not cross

Do not duplicate into Unzip City:

- Foundry War design material;
- the Semantic Tinkerer vomitorium;
- candidate scale-transition vocabulary such as Arboghasting;
- Crucible physical systems;
- game mechanics;
- the Tinkerer finish line.

Those remain source-local unless a future context-logistics pressure specifically requires them.

## Candidate evidence test

Do not manufacture a cold crossing merely to validate this interpretation.

When a real task next begins from a compact context surface and requires selective descent, observe whether the receiver can:

1. orient from a coarse representation;
2. identify the particular semantic boundary needing more resolution;
3. recover only enough richer material to resolve the pressure;
4. revise its working model from the source encounter;
5. compact outward without retaining irrelevant detail;
6. preserve a route back to the richer evidence;
7. act correctly from the revised coarse representation.

A failure at step 5 would be particularly interesting. It may reveal that retrieval is easy while **reformation after retrieval** remains manual or underspecified.

## Status

First reform complete.

No new context format, compiler, retrieval service, or repository topology is proposed.

The cargo exists because Semantic Tinkering exposed a genuine context-logistics question:

> **Can working context change resolution with attention while remaining recognizably the same recoverable material, and can consequences discovered at depth survive the return to a coarser working representation?**
