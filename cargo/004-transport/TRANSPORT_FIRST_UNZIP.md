## Source authority

This is a deliberately lossy reform of the completed Crucible Transport Expedition.

Authoritative source:

- `bonoj/Crucible/research/TRANSPORT_EXPEDITION.md`
- present executable truth: `bonoj/Crucible/SEMANTIC_SURFACE.md`

Transport is closed at Crucible commit `8c71bc8e`. The semantic surface was reconciled immediately before close at `2a4e9a1e`.

This cargo does not replace either source. It preserves what appears worth routing after the local expedition ended and identifies residue that should remain local or unresolved.

## What crossed

Transport began as a question about movement/representation and ultimately earned executable shallow water rather than merely another scalar visualization.

The durable physical result is an independent depth-averaged shallow-water system over mutable Crucible terrain. Solver state and presentation state are deliberately distinct. Hydrostatic reconstruction, wet/dry behavior, material-boundary handling, exact-terrain presentation clipping, and a calmer reconstructed visual skin now coexist without requiring render polygons to become hydrodynamic authority.

The accepted presentation is unlit transparent water, look L (`#17636a`, opacity `0.42`), with the original momentum-damping baseline retained. Broad A-Z appearance and 1-26 damping auditions were useful temporary machinery; their controls were stowed while the cheap tuning vocabulary remained latent.

Transport also produced a stronger experimental method. Competing explanations were preserved until executable observation discriminated them. Taran Wanderer names the ability to move observation across useful abstraction levels. Aeon Chronicler names preservation of actual competing and failed realities. The permanent 🐉 aperture demonstrated controlled counterfactual rendering against the same live geometry and, importantly, demonstrated that the instrument itself must remain falsifiable.

A genuine downward triangle-winding defect was discovered and corrected, but the visible pathology survived. The first Aeon interpretation was then falsified because its isolated render had accidentally removed scene lights and framebuffer alpha had been treated as water coverage. The corrected aperture preserved lights and used exact ray-confirmed water intersection. That evidence showed the lit transparent material could become dark/brown while an unlit counterfactual of the same geometry retained the expected water color. Production therefore moved to an unlit surface without changing the solver.

This sequence is useful residue because it preserves a collaboration rule rather than a water-specific fix:

**A real defect is not necessarily the causal defect, and a useful instrument must be able to falsify both the hypothesis and itself.**

## High-concept residue that escaped Transport

The fieldwork did not remain purely hydraulic.

Conversation around persistent witnesses, alternate realities, changing local constants, observer freedom, and eventually an ontology-morphing game converged on a separate experiment: a disembodied locus with a drive to become a weapon of quantum destruction, unable to be commanded and required to earn embodiment and capability from Crucible's own substrate.

That branch was correctly split rather than forced into Transport:

- `bonoj/Crucible/research/WQD_EXPEDITION.md`

The useful logistics fact is not the fictional weapon itself. It is that high-concept wandering can remain adjacent to executable fieldwork until a branch earns enough independent pressure to become its own local expedition. Unzip City does not need to pre-route such branches.

### Unzip City retains

Unzip City retains only the reform:

- Transport is an example of a local expedition producing both executable residue and methodological residue;
- temporary UI/instrumentation can expose a vocabulary and then disappear;
- counterfactual executable observation can be a semantic-JIT debugging instrument;
- failed hypotheses and instrument falsification are valuable provenance;
- conceptual branches should split only after they earn independent pressure;

### No destination earned yet

Transport does **not** currently justify moving water machinery, Aeon, Taran, Semantic JIT, or expedition logging into TabulaRasa, Accessibility, Clara, DigitalFamiliar, or another shared repository.

The patterns may later be reusable. Reuse pressure should trigger selective descent into this cargo and Crucible rather than preemptive framework extraction.

This follows the existing Unzip City result:

**orientation sufficiency is not mutation sufficiency.**

## Deliberately unfinished

Transport closed with an open performance seam.

On the tested mobile device, large wetted worlds tend toward approximately 15–20 FPS. The final discriminating aperture was not run. A future performance crossing should correlate frame rate with wet-cell count, solver time, presentation/reconstruction time, generated triangle count, and visible water coverage before choosing whether to attack solver work, reconstruction, or transparent fill/overdraw.

The legacy bounded sea also remains provisional. The new water system appears capable of subsuming its useful role, but no explicit subsumption/removal pass established that yet.

The Transport record itself has a provenance blemish: one logging write duplicated historical material and the final rendering/tuning sequence was not captured verbatim turn-for-turn. The expedition close explicitly records this rather than manufacturing a repaired chronology.

These are source-local leftovers, not cargo failures.

## Reform result

Transport supports the existing Unzip City process shape:

**context → expedition → evidence → reform → residue**

The strongest new refinement is:

**temporary machinery may disappear while the vocabulary it exposed survives.**

The A-Z and 1-26 controls are the simplest example. Aeon's schema is a stronger one: the valuable persistence is not one fixed diagnostic payload but the ability to construct the smallest executable counterfactual that discriminates the realities currently alive.

A second refinement is:

**branch late.**

The WQD line did not require a predefined repository category. It remained conversational residue until it became a coherent experiment, then earned its own local expedition while preserving provenance back to Transport.

## Status

Cargo 004 is a reform, not a new expedition.

No cold crossing is manufactured. No shared framework is extracted. No destination repository is mutated.

Transport has been unzipped far enough to answer what goes where:

**physical truth stays in Crucible; methodological residue stays recoverable here; independently earned experiments branch locally; unresolved engineering seams stay attached to the system that owns them.**
