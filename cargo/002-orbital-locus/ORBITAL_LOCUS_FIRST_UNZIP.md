## Present working model

The orbital station is a permanent Crucible fixture and a bounded observation locus, not a god object.

Its epistemic chain was earned incrementally:

**authoritative world → projected footprint → bounded aperture measurement → immutable observation → optional blind derivation → inference crossing**

The station's current scene-summary aperture is intentionally weaker than unrestricted world access. It samples bounded terrain morphology and explicitly available spatial evidence. Hidden world history, causal labels, objectives, personality instructions, and unrestricted ECS truth do not automatically cross.

The world beneath the locus can become richer independently of the locus. Meteors, deformable terrain, high-count granular matter, and an autonomous extruder can create persistent consequences without thereby becoming station knowledge.

A recurring implementation discipline is to keep world truth, spatial availability, measurement, derived evidence, and inference distinct.

## Earned machinery and why it exists

### Footprint and bounded observation

A generic projected footprint was introduced before richer sensing. Ordinary entities can opt into bounded spatial availability through explicit contracts; terrain remains substrate rather than being forced into an occupant abstraction.

The first aperture records bounded evidence on a cadence and freezes it as observations with provenance. A downstream analyzer receives observations only and has no route back to terrain, renderer, meteor history, or world state.

Controlled probes and CI tests were later added to separate derivation correctness from claims about live browser/world evidence. This followed an explicit miss where “testable” nearly became “tested.”

### Locus ledger and inference parcel

Natural observation history became a portable ledger. A pure inference-parcel projection can expose a bounded recent window of that evidence while omitting arbitrary extra source fields and hidden world state.

This allowed independently instantiated inference encounters to be studied without granting them the ongoing Crucible collaboration context.

### Granular spatial availability

High-count bearings could not honestly become one batch-wide occupant and could not afford per-bearing observation queries. Their authoritative integration therefore produces a cheap 64×64 density projection while doing work the population already owes.

The aperture samples only cells intersecting its footprint. Identity, authoritative bearing count, and the semantic label “ball bearing” do not cross.

Candidate reusable seam:

**expensive world truth → cheap world-owned spatial availability → bounded aperture measurement**

### Autonomous world process

The extruder was added specifically to make the Terrordrome richer without making the sensor smarter. It moves, excavates, emits bearings, and participates in ordinary world contracts without querying or serving the station.

Its repair history reinforced authority boundaries: physical emitters needed a physical single-bearing spawn seam rather than a population-construction API, and entity facing had to be written to authoritative ECS Transform state rather than a presentation object that render sync would overwrite.

### World-owned consequences and visibility

Meteor effects use a generic impact consequence seam rather than bearing-specific meteor knowledge.

Later Cinnabar packing exposed a similar ownership principle for visibility: below-plinth occlusion became a world/terrain-owned boundary instead of per-object hiding behavior.

These are separate mechanisms, but both were earned by moving responsibility toward the layer that owns the truth.

## Crossings: what bounded experience did and did not support

### Crossing 001

154 chronological observations were handed to a fresh inference without hidden meteor history.

The inference recovered substantial instrument and morphology structure but organized accumulated consequence as comparatively static geography. It over-promoted bounded evidence toward an authoritative account.

**Earned pressure:** meaningful spatial interpretation can arise from bounded evidence while omitted temporal cause remains unavailable.

### Crossing 002

232 observations through the same aperture, still without meteor history, produced a materially more temporal account: initially flat terrain becoming rougher/high-relief over time.

The hidden meteor process was still not recovered. The response also miscounted the evidence set despite using other correct extrema and timestamps.

**Earned pressure:** a longer bounded biography changed what kind of account could be constructed without adding a sensor. Interpretation quality and faithful bookkeeping remained separable.

### Crossing 003

After granular spatial availability was added, the inference noticed the new density channel but conflated it with a recurrent bounded occupant. It treated coexistence of two independent measurements as if they described one tracked object.

Hidden truth distinguished them: the bounded occupant was the extruder when present; granular density was distributed matter with multiple possible causes.

**Earned pressure:** richer evidence can create new opportunities for false association. The existing evidence already contained potential falsification, so the miss did not earn another aperture.

## Collaboration evidence

The record repeatedly shows a low-bandwidth human/model loop:

**language → implementation → physical consequence → human perception → revised language → new implementation**

Human input often consisted of semantic boundaries, live perceptual reports, acceptance/rejection, or descriptions of experienced friction rather than code-level prescriptions.

As shared context accumulated, useful continuation sometimes required very little new human information, including one-symbol continuation. The record explicitly does **not** claim that the underlying model learned or that this effect survives context loss. It records only that the experienced collaboration changed.

Several implementation failures were resolved by following authority rather than compensating locally:

- clipped station shadow → light shadow volume;
- “testable” vs “tested” → pure CI derivation plus separate live-world claim;
- wrong bearing performance → restore once-per-rendered-frame population integration;
- successful source/build but broken deployment → inspect exact generated artifact;
- phantom `lights.pulse` → remove nonexistent capability rather than invent it;
- extruder facing → authoritative Transform rather than presentation root;
- packed geometry visible below plinth → world-owned occlusion boundary.

A temporary 25-vehicle in-world design yard also demonstrated a collaboration shortcut: generate a bounded family in the actual executable environment, inspect simultaneously, select spatially, preserve useful surplus, and return to simulation.

## Compaction evidence

The orbital-launch-loop exchange crossed from Crucible into Clara and changed representation.

Crucible preserved the event because the event itself was experimental history. Clara retained a narrower locally relevant extraction and provenance back to Crucible rather than copying the chronology.

Candidate mechanism observed in practice:

**experience → preserve evidence → encounter a new context → recover what matters → compact locally → retain provenance**

Candidate principle:

> Compaction isn't primarily deleting detail. It's allowing experience to cross a boundary in the form useful on the other side.

No generalized compaction algorithm produced this. It remains an observed crossing, not proof that explicit continuity/memory machinery is unnecessary.

## Cinnabar and Cinnamon adjacency

The orbital record later became the chronological bag of holding for Cinnabar and Cinnamon as well.

The useful orbital-adjacent evidence is not the game fiction itself but several mechanisms it forced:

- scored chronology can become executable deterministic structure;
- presentation can be packed while persistent world consequences remain;
- a stable access surface can survive changing internal affordances;
- world ownership can resolve visibility boundaries;
- a human can communicate interface failures experientially while the model maps them onto different implementation layers.

Cinnabar and Cinnamon has its own dedicated field document for game state; the orbital chronology retains the surrounding collaboration evidence.

## Malleable locus surface and JIT accessibility

A small station control for stowing Cinnabar presentation underwent repeated live refinement: tap target, panel relationship, unified surface, constraint-derived layout, temporal interpolation, and below-plinth occlusion.

An older Continuity Lab interface supplied structural precedent: strong persistent outer frame, coherent subdivisions, shared margins/gutters, and justified controls. Its literal visual treatment was not the important inheritance.

This produced a candidate interface grammar:

**stable bounded surface → constraint-governed internal regions → provisional affordances**

The human then recognized the accessibility implication. Rather than selecting a pre-authored accessible variant, experienced friction could become conversational evidence that causes a constrained interface adaptation.

Candidate:

**human friction → conversational evidence → constrained interface adaptation**

The record explicitly preserves baseline accessibility, semantic roles, safe interaction bounds, and platform machinery as guardrails. It does not establish unconstrained model-generated UI or autonomous salience policy.

This material currently remains research inside the active Crucible/Unzip City work. It does not imply that an Accessibility repository or a SemanticJIT repository should own it.

## What is current versus historical

Current useful truths from this projection:

- the station is a permanent bounded locus;
- world truth and locus evidence remain deliberately distinct;
- the world can grow richer without automatically improving sensing;
- no second aperture has been earned by the first three crossings;
- granular matter exposes bounded spatial availability without per-bearing observational identity;
- the extruder is accepted autonomous world behavior;
- provenance-preserving reform/compaction has occurred manually across repository boundaries;
- CLARA's surface has supplied evidence for constrained malleable UI and JIT accessibility;
- Crucible remains the active workspace for these still-malleable ideas.

Historical material that should not silently become current architecture includes superseded implementation attempts, unaccepted candidates, early sensor proposals, and any speculative repository routing discussed before executable pressure earned it.

## Cold-entry recovery map

A receiving model should descend into source material only as needed.

Use the authoritative orbital chronology when:
- exact wording or turn order matters;
- an interpretation needs to be audited;
- a failure/correction chain matters;
- the strength of an evidence claim matters;
- a later projection appears to contradict history.

Use Crucible `SEMANTIC_SURFACE.md` when:
- present executable architecture/truth is needed.

Use crossing evidence under `research/evidence/continuity/` when:
- exact OUTSIDE responses or post-hoc resolution matter.

Use Cinnabar and Cinnamon's dedicated record when:
- current game chronology/state matters rather than orbital collaboration history.

Use Clara's `research/CRUCIBLE_ORBITAL_LOCUS.md` only for Clara's locally relevant extraction; it is not authoritative for the orbital chronology.
